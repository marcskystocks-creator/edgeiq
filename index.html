MEASUREMENT PROTOCOL — apply it the same way every single time
· Read it off the 1-MINUTE chart, WICKS not closes. A 5-minute candle hides the spike that would have taken your stop, and that spike is the whole point of MAE.
· MAE — worst price against you, in points from your entry fill. MFE — best price in your favour. Both measured entry fill to exit fill, nothing before, nothing after.
· RAN ON — furthest it went your way after you were out. Window closes 30 minutes after your exit, or at the end of the killzone you traded in, whichever comes first. Exit 10:46 in Silver Bullet → measure to 11:00, not 11:16.
· Log it even when the target hit — that is the case it matters most. Target hit with a long run means the target is too tight; target hit and it reverses means the target was right. You cannot tell those apart without this number.
· If it never went further your way, type 0. Zero is a finding. Blank is missing data.
· All three are positive numbers. Never change this rule mid-collection — these figures are only used comparatively, so one rule applied consistently beats a better rule applied unevenly.

THE STOP BUYS THE SIZE
the cap you will actually hit
One trade may risk at most 20% of the daily loss limit — $300. That budget divided by the stop decides how many contracts you can afford. The ladder cap and the risk cap both apply and the smaller one wins. The contract dropdown will not open past the risk cap until the stop is entered. Define the stop, then size — not the other way round.
MNQ STOPRISK PER LOTCONTRACTS THE BUDGET BUYS
This is why a 10-contract ceiling is not a 10-contract licence. Ten lots needs a stop near 7.5 points. Friday's 43.75-point stop affords three, whatever Panel P reads.
TUNE IT
ACCOUNTS tab · not the source
Every number on this page is derived from the account selected for the day — its buffer sets the session stop, the session stop sets the per-trade budget, and the budget divided by your stop sets the size. Change the account and the whole page follows. The only figure you set by hand is your own session stop, on the ACCOUNTS tab; everything else comes from the catalog. The firm's maximum is a ceiling, not a risk control — across every product in the catalog it permits between 50% and 83% of the account on a single trade. Yours is the number that binds.

IMPORT BROKER FILLS
TradingView Orders.csv · TopstepX Orders or Trades
Load a broker file and it is paired into round trips, so a 5-lot entry stays a 5-lot trade. Entry and exit times, size, direction, fill prices and P&L are computed from the file — no typing, nothing from memory. Three shapes are understood: TradingView / Tradeify Orders.csv, TopstepX Orders (both fill-level, paired FIFO), and TopstepX Trades (already matched, taken as given). An initial stop is picked up only when a protective stop sat on the losing side of the entry; a trailing stop is not risk and is ignored, and a matched-trade file carries no stops at all.
What the file cannot tell you, it will not guess. Panel P at entry, F2, setup, trigger and location import blank and show as soft flags. Tap an imported row to fill them in. That is the judgement half of the log and it is the half the analytics need.
—
Trades land on the dates in the file, not on the day you are viewing. One file is one account. Re-loading replaces only that account's imported trades on those dates — the other account's rows and anything you typed by hand are left alone. That is what lets a two-broker day go in as two files.
EXCEL HANDOFF
FUTURES LOG tab, cell A5
Copy for Excel puts every row on the clipboard as tab-separated text. Open EDGEIQ_Master_Journal_v4.xlsx, go to FUTURES LOG, tap cell A5, paste. Excel splits it into columns A–S on its own — no file to download, no Text to Columns.
THIS EXPORT IS 19 COLUMNS, A–S, ON PURPOSE
· U, V and W in that sheet are formulas — DAY P&L RUNNING, LOSS LIMIT REMAINING, CUMULATIVE P&L
· a wider paste at A5 would overwrite all three and break the workbook
· every field added after v3 goes in FULL EXPORT below instead
Paste over the whole block every time. The export is always full history, never just the new day, so appending double-counts every prior session. DASHBOARD, BEHAVIOR and DAILY recalculate themselves.
If you download the CSV instead, open it in Excel first so it parses into columns, then copy from there. Copying the raw file text drops everything into column A.
